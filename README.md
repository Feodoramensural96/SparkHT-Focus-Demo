## ⚙️ Understanding How It Works

The application detects your focus levels using a vision model. It processes the video feed in real-time. When it notices a change in your behavior or environment, it sends a notification or visual cue. This feedback allows you to adjust your posture or return to your primary task. The system prioritizes local processing to ensure your data stays on your machine.

## 🛠 Troubleshooting Common Issues

If you encounter problems, check these areas first.

*   Application does not start: Verify your Windows updates. Ensure your antivirus does not block the application.
*   Camera access fails: Open your Windows privacy settings. Make sure you allow desktop apps to access your camera.
*   Notifications are missing: Check the Windows Action Center. Ensure 'Do Not Disturb' mode is off.
*   High CPU usage: Close other heavy applications while working. The recognition model requires a portion of your system resources to run accurately.

## 🔒 Privacy and Security

Data privacy remains a core principle of this project. The application analyzes video frames locally. It does not send your personal video footage to remote servers. All recognition happens on your own hardware. Your activity logs stay within your local storage folder. You can clear these logs at any time through the application settings menu.

We built this tool to encourage healthy work habits. Rather than forcing productivity, the application offers gentle suggestions. Use the logs to identify your peak performance hours. Disable or enable specific alerts to match your personal testing style.
